Derived portions: GPU ping-pong height/velocity water simulation; cosine-profile disturbances; Snell-law ray propagation; differential-area caustic focusing. The integration adapts these algorithms to variable physical time steps, selectable simulation resolution, monochrome underwater rendering, actual receiving planes, and the supplied live flower.

The original Three.js runtime, Stats, OrbitControls, OBJLoader, skybox, shark, rock and plant assets are not used by the delivered `index.html`. Original upstream source is retained in `reference/` for comparison; its external demo dependencies are not dependencies of this artwork.
